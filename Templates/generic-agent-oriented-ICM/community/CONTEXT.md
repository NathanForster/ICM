# community CONTEXT

## Workspace Mission

This workspace owns outward-facing communication: outreach, marketing,
customer communication, and social content. It is the authoritative location
for campaign copy, announcements, and communication records.

## Allowed

- create and modify communication files in this workspace
- generate posts, announcements, and campaign deliverables
- update outreach workflow state

## Never

- publish or send externally without human review
- include confidential, unreleased, or speculative information
- make commitments (dates, prices, features) not confirmed by the human operator
- modify unrelated workspaces
