# Risk Management

Engineering risk tracking.

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| [risk-register.md](risk-register.md) | Master risk register — likelihood × impact scoring, mitigations, owners |

Add risks as they are identified; review at the cadence stated in the register.
An AI agent noticing a new project risk (unstable dependency, ambiguous requirement,
schedule conflict) should propose a register entry rather than leaving it unrecorded.
