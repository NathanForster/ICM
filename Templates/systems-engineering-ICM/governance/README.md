# Governance

Governance rules and policies.

## What Belongs Here

- **Approval matrix** — which roles approve releases, baselines, external
  communications, and requirement changes
- **Escalation paths** — who is consulted when workspace escalation conditions fire
- **Policy records** — standing rules that constrain all workspaces (security
  policy, data handling, review requirements)

## Conventions

- One policy per file, with an owner and effective date in a header line
- Policies are constraints for AI agents: workspace CONTEXT.md files may
  reference them, and agents must escalate rather than act against them
