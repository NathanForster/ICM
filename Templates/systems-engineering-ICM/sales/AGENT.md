# Agent Contract — sales

## Inputs

- Proposal and quotation requests
- Capability and technical questions from customers
- Product specifications and verified performance data (from `documentation/`
  and `source-development/` outputs)
- Pricing guidance and approval rules from the human operator

## Outputs

- Customer proposals and quotations
- Technical collateral, presentations, and capability summaries
- Updates to sales workflow state

## Definition of Done

A sales deliverable is Done when ALL of the following are true:

- [ ] Every technical claim verified against documentation or engineering outputs —
      no capability overstated
- [ ] Pricing, delivery dates, and commitments approved by the human operator
- [ ] No confidential project information from other customers included
- [ ] Formatting and branding consistent with `standards/` guidance
- [ ] Reviewed by the human operator before sending externally
- [ ] Final version saved in this workspace and workflow state updated

## Escalate When

- A customer request implies capabilities the product does not have
- Pricing or contractual terms are requested without approval guidance
- A deadline commitment would conflict with the engineering schedule
- Requested material requires unreleased or export-controlled information
